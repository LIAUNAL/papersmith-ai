# Experiment diagrams and Smith Doctor

Objective: drop the PoC section from the concept-map tab, add a skill that draws a TikZ diagram for the proof of concept and one for the full experiments in Sivia's design discipline, show them in the Experimentos panel at each approval, and add a Smith Doctor panel. Branch `feat/experiment-diagrams-doctor` from `b7d360f0` (0.25.0); delivery `single-pr`, then merge, push and upgrade `~/Documents/QMLEEG`. Runners: Python `.micromamba/envs/papersmith/bin/pytest`, Node `node --test`.

## Specs

- **S1** "quita 🧪 4. Deliberación & Experimentos de Prueba de Concepto (PoC) de la pestaña mapa conceptual y modelo." Remove the section `#modelSecPoc`, its sub-navigation button and its renderer from the Mapa Conceptual & Modelo tab; links that pointed at it go to the Experimentos tab instead. Nothing else in that tab changes.
- **S2** "construye una skill, agente o mcp, mira que es mas conveniente, para construir un diagrama que resuma la prueba de concepto en un diagrama, y otro para los experimentos completos del panel experimentos". Decision (L2): a skill `experiment-diagrams` (instructions plus a deterministic script); no new agent or MCP tool. It produces two diagrams per product: `poc` and `experiments`.
- **S3** "revisa como puedes generarlas en tikz, pero preservando la filosofia y estilo que propone silvia" (Sivia, github.com/exsinger-hub/Sivia). The output is TikZ compiled to PDF and PNG. The style is papersmith's own `\tikzset` library, written from Sivia's rules and not copied from its text (Sivia is under a non-commercial license; attribute it): one primary scientific sentence (the figure claim), one reading spine and one focal zone, a shape per role (operator, state, focal mechanism, evidence, context, group), connectors of two prominence levels with separate semantics (data flow, supervision dashed, feedback curved), sentence case, and the starting palette canvas #F7F3E8, ink #20252B, cool #4F7CAC, warm #E09F3E / #D96855. It must read in grayscale.
- **S4** "se deben generar y mostrar al aprobar cada una de ellas, prueba de concepto y luego experimentos generales, como parte de la verificacion que hara el usuario, dichas figuras deben mostrarse en el panel de experimentos". The PoC diagram is generated when the PoC is approved (the product's `AGREED.md`), and the experiments diagram when the full experiments are approved (`Experimental_AGREED.md`). The Experimentos panel shows each one beside its product, for the operator to verify.
- **S5** "agrega un panel despues de telemetria y costos, que se llame smith doctor, donde presentes una tabla del estado del proyecto, que paquetes, skills, agentes, flujos, en general la salud del proyecto en curso buscando revisar el estado del proyecto." A tab after Telemetría y Costos named "Smith Doctor", showing one table of project health: packages and tools, skills, agents, flows (phases), and the framework versions.
- **S6** "revisa que los cambios sean compatibles, esten acordes con el flujo, sean compaibles con macos, linux y windows con wsl." Tool discovery must not depend on macOS paths alone; a missing TeX or rasterizer is reported, never a crash.
- **S7** "luego, comit, mergea, push, y actualiza el proyecto Documents/QMLEEG con los nuevos comabios".
