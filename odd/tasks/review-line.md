# Review line: PRISMA 2020 systematic review papers beside the experimental line

Objective: `smith init --kind review` creates a workspace for a systematic review paper (no experiments) that follows PRISMA 2020; the existing experimental line stays byte-for-byte as it is.
Branch: feat/review-line (from main 0.75.0, worktree ../papersmith-ai-worktrees/review-line). Delivery: ask-on-risk (forecast above 400 lines; chain strategy asked before any PR). Test runner: pytest (`.venv/bin/python -m pytest`; the worktree links the main checkout's `.venv` and `node_modules`, both gitignored).

## Specs

- S1 (Request) "dale con prisma 2020 como estrategia para ese tipo de papers y ajusta el flujo pero recuerda que es una linea distinta, no vamos a cambiar la linea que ya tenemos, solo poner una linea alterna cuando es de revision"
- S2 The experimental line does not change: `smith init` without `--kind` (or `--kind experimental`) and `smith upgrade` on an existing workspace produce the same files and bytes as before; review-only kit files never reach an experimental workspace.
- S3 `smith init --kind review` records `paper_kind: "review"` in `.papersmith/config.json`; `smith upgrade`, `smith status` and `smith audit --check-drift` read it back and keep the review layout (review section contracts in `sections/`, no experimental section contracts restored).
- S4 Review workspaces get a review flow (the harness entrypoints' "Paper construction flow" names the PRISMA 2020 phases instead of the experimental ones) and review section contracts: title, abstract (PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts), introduction with objectives/RQs, methods (protocol and registration, eligibility criteria, information sources, search strategy, selection process, data collection, risk of bias, synthesis methods), results (study selection with the PRISMA flow diagram, study characteristics, risk of bias, results per RQ), discussion (taxonomy, challenges, future directions), limitations, conclusions, and the experimental back matter shared unchanged; registration is reported in the methods and the abstract (the `review-registration` declaration) and the PRISMA checklist is the supplementary file `review_cli.py checklist` reports.
- S5 A `systematic-review` skill (review workspaces only) runs the PRISMA 2020 stages, all keyless and reproducible, under `review/`: protocol (RQs with PICOC, per-source search strings, date range, inclusion/exclusion criteria, registration); identification (query OpenAlex, Crossref and Europe PMC, import RIS/BibTeX exports from Scopus/WoS/IEEE, log date, source, query and count of every search, deduplicate by DOI then normalized title); screening (title/abstract and full-text decisions with exclusion reasons, two reviewers, Cohen's kappa); risk of bias / quality appraisal; data extraction; PRISMA counts, the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram and the checklist status; basic bibliometrics (per year, per venue, keyword co-occurrence).
- S7 (Request) "dale con la síntesis comparativa y ajusta los contratos, recuerda que no debes tocar las reglas para papers convencionales como tenemos, solo si se escoge paper de revision": the review contracts follow the group's MDPI review A029 (Background section, thematic results as a comparative synthesis with bold-labelled sub-themes, a closing synthesis sentence and a comparison table per theme), carry a measured `## LIA house style` and `## Disqualifiers`, and the experimental `sections/` stay byte-identical.
- S6 (assumption) The command center's phase board stays experimental in this feature; a review-aware dashboard is a later feature.
